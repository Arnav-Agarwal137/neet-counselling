# NEET Counselling Toolkit

**Six tools for the part nobody prepares you for: after the result.**

You spend two years learning to get the rank. Then the rank arrives, and you are
handed MCC and KEA counselling, thirty-page information bulletins, closing-rank
tables nobody explains, a document list you get one chance at, and about a week
to make a decision you live with for five and a half years.

These are the tools I built for my own counselling. Free, no login, and they work
on a phone.

## The main one: **Where You Get In**

### **→ [Open it](https://arnav-agarwal137.github.io/neet-counselling/where-you-get-in/)** · also live at [neet-planner-shared.web.app](https://neet-planner-shared.web.app)

Everyone builds a rank predictor. **This is the only one I know of that assumes
your family is in the room** — because in Indian counselling, they are.

**It does two things.**

**1. It tells you where you actually get in.** Not a table to scroll — every
college sorts itself into **You get in · Borderline · Out**, against your rank,
your category, your quota and your domicile. Borderline is its own category on
purpose: the honest answer for a lot of colleges is *"depends how this year goes"*,
and a tool that pretends otherwise is lying to you.

**2. It lets the people advising you actually advise you.** You share your list,
and your family can **reply on individual colleges, and counter each other.** The
reply box literally prompts you with *"e.g. Amma, Ravi, Dr. Rao"*. Share it
straight to **WhatsApp**, which is where this conversation happens anyway.

That is the part that makes it different. Every other predictor hands one person
a list. **Counselling decisions are not made by one person.**

**Also in it:** per-college **facilities, location and campus**, with a map view ·
**dark mode** · everything **saved between visits** · **search across every
college** · works on a phone, because that is where you will actually open it
during counselling.

**11 MB of embedded data, 115 functions, 309 references to rounds, quotas,
categories and domicile.** The weight is the cutoff data, and the data is the
point.

## The other five

### Rank Finder
A lighter, instant version — type a rank, get a **You Get In / Out of Range**
split with live counts on each. Built around **KCET rank 1300** as the worked
example.

### NIRF Karnataka College Guide
Every MBBS college a Karnataka general-domicile candidate can reach, with
**NIRF 2025 ranking, fees, bond terms and your own notes** against each.
**Search, filter by AIQ or KEA, sort any column, keyboard shortcuts**, and a real
**print-to-PDF** layout so you can carry it into the hall on paper.

### State Medical Colleges — Full Guide 2026
The complete Karnataka state-quota list, searchable, with a detail panel per
college. **Print-ready.**

### AIIMS & Central Colleges
AIIMS, BHU, MAMC, VMMC and the rest of the central pool — **type a name and it
filters as you go.**

### Master Document Checklist
Every document MCC and KEA ask for, as a checklist that **remembers your ticks
between visits** and prints onto one page. **Arriving without a document is the
avoidable way people lose a seat.**

## Honest limits

- Cutoffs are **from past years** and move every season. A guide, never a
  guarantee — **check the official MCC or KEA bulletin before you lock a choice.**
- **Karnataka-centric** by construction.
- I am a NEET candidate who built tools for himself, **not a counsellor.** Nothing
  here is official advice.
