# NEET Counselling Toolkit

**Five tools for the part nobody prepares you for: after the result.**

You spend two years learning to get the rank. Then the rank arrives, and you are
handed MCC and KEA counselling, thirty-page information bulletins, closing-rank
tables nobody explains, a document list you get one chance at, and about a week
to make a decision you live with for five and a half years.

These are the tools I built for my own counselling. They are live, free, and work
on a phone.

### **→ [Open the toolkit](https://arnav-agarwal137.github.io/neet-counselling/)**

---

## What is in it

### 1. Rank Finder — *"with my rank, where do I actually get in?"*
Enter a rank and every college sorts itself into **You Get In** and **Out of
Range**, with a live count on each. No scrolling a PDF looking for your number.
Built and demonstrated around **KCET rank 1300**.

### 2. NIRF Karnataka College Guide
Every MBBS college a Karnataka general-domicile candidate can reach, with
**NIRF 2025 ranking, fees, bond terms and your own notes** against each one.
**Search, filter by AIQ or KEA, sort by any column, keyboard shortcuts, and a
clean print-to-PDF layout** so you can take it into the counselling hall on paper.

### 3. State Medical Colleges — Full Guide 2026
The complete Karnataka state-quota list, searchable, with a detail panel per
college. **Print-ready.**

### 4. AIIMS & Central Colleges
AIIMS, BHU, MAMC, VMMC and the rest of the central pool in one searchable place —
**type a college name and it filters as you go.**

### 5. Master Document Checklist
Every document MCC and KEA ask for, as a checklist that **remembers what you have
ticked between visits** and prints onto one page. The one thing that actually
loses people a seat is arriving without a document, and it is entirely avoidable.

## Honest limits

- Cutoffs are **from past years** and shift every season. Treat them as a guide,
  never a guarantee — **always check the official MCC or KEA bulletin** before
  you lock a choice.
- Karnataka-centric by construction.
- I am a NEET candidate who built tools for himself, **not a counsellor.**
  Nothing here is official advice.
