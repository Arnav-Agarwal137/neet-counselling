Why AIIMS & Central Colleges Deserve Preference at the Same Rank
No Bond (All AIIMS)Zero post-MBBS bond obligation. State colleges at the same cutoff tier mandate 1–3 years rural service.
Internal PG Quota — 50%BHU, AMU, and DU-affiliated colleges (MAMC, UCMS, LHMC) reserve 50% of PG MD/MS seats for their own MBBS graduates.
Internship StipendAIIMS: ₹17,500–20,000/mo. Central univ: ₹14,000–16,000/mo. Karnataka state colleges: ₹8,000–12,000/mo.
Fees Are NominalAIIMS: ₹1,628/yr. Central univ: ₹12,000–28,000/yr. Karnataka state govt (BMCRI etc.): ₹68,000–75,000/yr.
Central InfrastructureAMU, BHU, DU colleges receive central budget allocation. Consistently better equipment, wards, and library than equivalent state colleges.
INI-CET AdvantageAIIMS MBBS graduates compete in INI-CET for PG — a smaller, less crowded pool than NEET-PG's 2 lakh+ applicants.
