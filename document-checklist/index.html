First — the Bengaluru trip check: per KEA’s 1 Aug notice, in-person verification is only for reservation/NRI/minority claims and certain special categories. Candidates who already downloaded the verification slip, and newly registered candidates claiming no reservation, are not called — as a GM candidate with no claims, your whole “verification” may just be (re)downloading the slip.

How many photocopies in total? KEA’s official verification list asks for one photocopy set; MCC-side guides say 2–3 self-attested sets, some advise 3–5. The safe play across BOTH systems plus college admission: originals + five self-attested sets + 8–10 photos (the same photo as the NEET form, name and roll number written lightly on the back), plus a clear PDF scan of everything in one cloud folder — with 20–500 KB versions ready for the MCC portal, and DigiLocker linked.

Formats are law: the final word on any certificate’s exact format is the annexure pages of the MCC Information Bulletin 2026 and KEA’s Information Booklet (17 Jan 2026). If any row here ever disagrees with those two PDFs, the PDFs win.
